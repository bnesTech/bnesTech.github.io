### Notice of Dissolution and Request for Filing of Claims

bnesTech Inc. (the “Company”) resolved to dissolve pursuant to the resolution
of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders held on December 1, 2025,
and a liquidator has been duly appointed.

All creditors of the Company are hereby requested to file their claims **within two (2) months**
from the date of this public notice.

Please be advised that any claim not filed within this period may be **excluded**
from the liquidation process.

- **Filing period**: Two (2) months from December 1, 2025
- **Submission & Contact**: Email to `bnestech@gmail.com`

Date: December 1, 2025

bnesTech Inc.
