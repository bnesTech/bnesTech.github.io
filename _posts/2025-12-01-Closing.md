### Notice of Dissolution and Request for Filing of Claims

bnesTech Inc. (the “Company”) resolved to dissolve at the Extraordinary General Meeting
of Shareholders held on December 1, 2025, and a liquidator has been duly appointed.

All creditors of the Company are hereby requested to file their claims **within two (2) months**
from the date of this public notice by email.

Please be advised that any claim not filed within this period may be
**excluded from the liquidation proceeding**.

- **Filing Period**: Two (2) months from December 1, 2025
- **Submission & Contact**: Email to `bnestech@gmail.com`

Date: December 1, 2025

bnesTech Inc.
